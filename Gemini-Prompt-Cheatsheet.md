# Gemini for Google Workspace: Prompting Cheatsheet

## What is Gemini for Workspace?
Gemini is your AI-powered assistant integrated into Google Workspace apps (Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Meet, Slides, and Gemini Advanced). Effective prompting helps you collaborate with Gemini to boost productivity and creativity without sacrificing privacy or security.

## Four Key Components of Effective Prompts

| Component | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Persona** | Who you are or your role | "I am a project manager..." |
| **Task** | The specific action needed | "Draft an email..." "Summarize..." |
| **Context** | Background information | "Based on last week's meeting..." |
| **Format** | Desired output structure | "In bullet points..." "As a table..." |

**Note:** You don't need all four in every prompt, but always include a verb/command (Task).

## Quick Tips for Effective Prompts

1. **Use natural language** - Write as if speaking to another person
2. **Be specific and iterate** - Provide context and refine with follow-up prompts
3. **Be concise** - State requests in brief but specific language
4. **Make it a conversation** - Fine-tune if results don't meet expectations
5. **Use your documents** - Tag files with @filename to provide context
6. **Start with action verbs** - Begin with clear commands (summarize, draft, create)

## Sample Prompts by App

### Gmail
- **Email draft**: "Draft an email to [person] about [topic]. Include [details] and use a [tone] tone."
- **Meeting recap**: "Summarize this email thread and list all action items and deadlines."

### Docs
- **Content creation**: "Create an outline for [topic] with sections for [key areas]."
- **Summarize**: "Summarize this document in two paragraphs focusing on [key points]."
- **Refinement**: "Make this paragraph more concise while maintaining key information."

### Sheets
- **Data analysis**: "Create a formula that calculates [specific calculation]."
- **Tracker creation**: "Create a project tracker with columns for [items to track]."

### Slides
- **Presentation help**: "Create a slide describing [topic] based on @[filename]."
- **Visual creation**: "Create an image of [description] using [style/colors]."

### Meet
- **Meeting assistance**: Use "Take notes with Gemini" to automatically capture key points

### Gemini Advanced
- **In-depth research**: "I am a [role]. Help me research [topic] focusing on [specific aspects]."
- **Brainstorming**: "Generate ideas for [project/task] considering [constraints/parameters]."

## Leveling Up Your Prompts

- **Break it up** - Split complex tasks into separate, sequential prompts
- **Give constraints** - Include specific parameters (word count, tone, style)
- **Assign a role** - Start with "You are a [role]..." to guide response style
- **Ask for feedback** - "What questions do you have that would help you provide better output?"
- **Specify tone** - Request formal, casual, technical, creative, etc.
- **Iterate** - If results aren't ideal, try rephrasing or refining your prompt

## Prompt Structure Example
```
I am a [your role]. 
I need to [task/action needed]. 
Use @[filename] to [specific instructions]. 
Format it as [desired format].
```

**Remember:** Always review AI-generated content before using. Gemini is meant to help, but the final output is yours.
